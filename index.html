Here are a few Demo Projects I've worked on:

Order Tracking CRM for an E-commerce Business

Problem: Payments and customer complaints were scattered, so it was hard to see what stage each order was at.

Solution: I built a HubSpot CRM with a contact database and an eight-stage deal pipeline: New Inquiry, Order Placed, Payment Confirmed, Packed & Ready, Out for Delivery, Delivered, Complaint Raised and Resolved. I added custom properties such as Last Order Date, Total Spend, Product Preference and Delivery Confirmed. I created a High Value segment for customers who spent over $1000 ready for targeted promotions.

Organized Customer Support Ticketing

Problem: Customer questions arrived through different channels, with no clear record of who was handling what or how quickly replies went out.

Solution: fully configured Freshdesk portal with ticket categories, SLA rules and auto-replies active. I managed customer tickets in one place: sorting by priority and status, writing clear replies, using canned responses for common questions, and updating tickets until each issue was resolved. The result was a cleaner inbox and more consistent response times.

Automating Repetitive Tasks with Zapier

I built a Zapier automation with a trigger for when a new Calendly interview is booked. Its first action automatically creates a new folder in Google Drive, named after the candidate. Its second action posts a message in the #interview-alerts Slack channel with the candidate's name, interview time and assigned recruiter.

Hassle-Free Appointment Booking

I set up Calendly syncing with Google Calendar, with different meeting types, available hours, buffer times, and automatic confirmation and reminder emails. Clients can now book a time that suits them in a few clicks, with no scheduling back-and-forth.
